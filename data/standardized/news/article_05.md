## Global study pathways
Pathway 1
Complete your entire degree in Vietnam (with campus exchange between the Hanoi City campus and the Saigon South campus available).
Pathway 2
Start at RMIT Vietnam and go on exchange for 1–2 semesters at RMIT Melbourne or partner universities worldwide (at Vietnam tuition fee rates).
Pathway 3
Start at RMIT Vietnam and transfer to RMIT Melbourne to complete your degree.
Pathway 4
Study your entire program at RMIT Melbourne, Australia.
