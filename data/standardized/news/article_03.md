## Overview
Careers Festival 2026 is built on the belief that career readiness is an ongoing process rather than a fixed destination—one that can be actively shaped over time. Delivered by the Careers, Alumni & Industry Relations team, the festival supports participants in building capability aligned with their individual goals, interests, and strengths. Through practical experiences and real‑world industry insights, CF 2026 aims to equip students with the skills, knowledge, and professional connections to thrive in an evolving world of work. To support this, the festival focuses on the following objectives:
### Build career confidence
Learn how to navigate today’s job market, understand industry expectations, and take practical steps toward your goals.
### Explore employability opportunities
Connect directly with companies and discover your various career pathways, internships, and job opportunities.
### Stand out as career-ready talent
Showcase your strengths to employers who are actively looking for well-prepared candidates.
### Expand your professional network
Engage with leading organizations and build connections that support your long-term career journey.
