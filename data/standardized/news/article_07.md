### If you're joining RMIT from out of town or overseas, or leaving home for the first time, we can help you find accommodation that suits your needs.
### Personalised accommodation support
Our dedicated team can help you with
  * Information on housing options that suit your needs
  * Advice on rental and tenancy issues
  * Information about moving in and moving out of your property


## Get in touch
### International students
RMIT’s friendly staff in both HCMC and Hanoi can recommend the best areas to live at a price that suits your budget.
### General enquiries
For general enquiries about accommodation, the residential support team is happy to assist.
### Current students
For current students seeking accommodation, we recommend contacting Student Connect.
