### Learn more about the Student Academic Success (SAS) services for current students.
Welcome to Student Academic Success (SAS), where you can find different kinds of support for your academic learning such as planning and feedback on assignments, using AI to study more efficiently and effectively, improving your understanding of the course content and more. Our goal is to help build your skills and strategies to help you achieve your individual study objectives. 
We provide learning support services to assist you in gaining academic success and skills-based development. Explore our Writing and Learning Centre and Peer-Assisted Learning services below. 
### [The Writing and Learning Centre ](https://www.rmit.edu.vn/students/support/student-academic-success/writing-and-learning-centre)
Drop in individually or with a group to get professional support from a Learning Advisor on your assignments and study. Also, look out for workshops for your course or program delivered by the Learning Advisors
### [Peer Assisted Learning ](https://www.rmit.edu.vn/students/support/student-academic-success/peer-assisted-learning-pal)
Get study support from a fellow student who has been academically successful and specifically trained by SAS to support you. Drop in to our service locations or book online for one-to-one support or check our schedule for workshops running for your courses.
### [Academic Success Advisors ](https://www.rmit.edu.vn/students/support/student-academic-success/academic-success-advising)
The Academic Success Advisor team are experts in helping at risk students get their studies back on the right path. If you are at risk, your ASA will invite you to goal setting session to help you get your studies back on track.
### [Online Resources ](https://learninglab.rmit.edu.au/#gsc.tab=0)
The RMIT Learning Lab is a collection of high-quality learning resources designed with you in mind. On the website, you can find a variety of tutorials, activities, and quizzes created for your self-study of core university skills as well as knowledge on how to study and do different assessment types.
