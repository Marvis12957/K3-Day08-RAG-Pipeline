## 2025 RMIT Vietnam Scholarships
Over 25 years in Vietnam, RMIT University has awarded scholarships to more than 1,840 Vietnamese and international students, exceeding a total value of 566 billion VND. These scholarships span over diverse categories, including full and partial scholarships and those specific to different programs. In 2025, RMIT continues to award high-achieving students with scholarships that have a total value of nearly 53.5 billion VND.
### Scholarships applied to programs at RMIT Vietnam
RMIT Vietnam Scholarships officially launched in January 2025 with more than 106 scholarships for Vietnamese and international students: 
  * 7 Vice-Chancellor's Scholars (covering 100% of the tuition fee)
  * 22 Academic Achievement Scholarships (covering 25 - 50% of the tuition fee)
  * Scholarships for specific programs (covering 50% of the tuition fee): 
    * 11 scholarships from The Business School
    * 8 scholarships from School of Communication & Design
    * 5 scholarships from School of Science, Engineering & Technology
  * And many others to discover


### Key dates
  * Applications open: 12 January 2025
  * Applications close: 4 July 2025, 5:00pm GMT +7
  * Release of outcome: 26 September 2025
