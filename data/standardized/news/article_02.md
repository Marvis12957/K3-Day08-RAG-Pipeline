## RMIT Tech Camp 2026
  * 15/04/2026 - 26/06/2026
  * 09:00am - 05:00pm 
  * Online and on campuses
  * RMIT Saigon South, RMIT Hanoi
  * Language: English


Fresh from the success of last year's tech camp, the School of Science, Engineering and Technology proudly presents RMIT Tech Camp 2026 - a dynamic program designed for high school students across Vietnam who are ready to explore, create and make an impact through technology. 
Over more than two weeks, participants will learn about artificial intelligence, embedded systems and cybersecurity from RMIT lecturers, develop a real-world project with their teammates and compete in the final round for prizes worth over VND 65,000,000.
## Join us
### Build future-ready skills
Learn and apply your knowledge, fully funded while exploring future-shaping areas such as Generative AI, machine learning, embedded systems and cybersecurity.
### Start building real-world projects in high school
Strengthen your portfolio and gain a meaningful edge when applying for university scholarships.
### Expand your network
Meet and connect with RMIT lecturers and industry partners in tech; experience the international university environment at RMIT firsthand.

## Platinum partners
Our Platinum Partners, Google and Amazon Web Services (AWS) along with Megazone Cloud, are among the world’s leading technology companies, bringing valuable industry insights and expertise to the program.
Through this partnership, finalists will have the opportunity to participate in exclusive offline workshop and receive exciting prizes as well as industry-supported learning opportunities.
## Awards
A certificate of completion will be provided to all participants who submit a final group project.
### The grand final
10 winning teams will be chosen per campus (RMIT Hanoi, RMIT Saigon South), with a total prize pool of VND 65,000,000+ including
First prize
value equivalent to VND 10,000,000 (in-kind and in gift vouchers)
First runner-up
value equivalent to VND 5,000,000 (in-kind and in gift vouchers)
Second runner-up
value equivalent to VND 3,000,000 (in-kind and in gift vouchers)
Fourth prize
value equivalent to VND 1,000,000 (in-kind and in gift vouchers)
