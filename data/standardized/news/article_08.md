## Student Connect operating hours
  * **Online Portal** : Log an enquiry 24/7 and we'll get back to you
  * **Webchat Service** : 8.30am-5.30pm, Monday-Friday, excluding public and university holidays
  * **Phone Service** : 8.30am-5.30pm, Monday-Friday, excluding public and university holidays 
    * +84 28 3776 1306 - Press 1
  * **Walk-in service** : 8.30am-5.30pm, Monday-Friday, excluding public and university holidays 
    * Hanoi Campus: Handi Resco Building - Floor 1 - Room 1.1.009
    * Saigon South Campus: Beanland Building - Ground floor - Student common area


During particularly busy times, our phone line and webchat may close earlier than the listed time. This ensures we can answer existing queued enquiries before the service closes at the time listed. Alternatively, you can submit an enquiry via the online portal any time, and we will respond as soon as possible.
