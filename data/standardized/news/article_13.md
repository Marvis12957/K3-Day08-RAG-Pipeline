## We care about your health at RMIT!
As your on‑campus health centre, we are here to support you throughout your student journey. Whether you're feeling unwell, stressed, overwhelmed, or simply need advice, our on‑campus clinics and 24/7 global support services are here to help.
All services are confidential and available to all RMIT Vietnam students.
### We support students with
  * General medical consultations
  * Treatment of common illnesses and minor injuries
  * Emergency triage and referral services
  * Specialist referrals, when required
  * Medical advice on health‑related matters


### We offer
  * RMIT health centres provide free services and are located at our Hanoi City and Saigon South campuses.
  * Students may walk in during clinic hours or book an appointment with the doctor of their choice.
  * All consultations are private, confidential, and free. Immediate on‑site treatment carries no additional charge.
