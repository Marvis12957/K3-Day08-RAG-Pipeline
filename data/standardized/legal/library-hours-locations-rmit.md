Saigon South campus

During semester

Monday to Friday: 8am - 9pm

Saturday and Sunday: 8am - 5pm

Exam Time (week 10 - 12): Monday - Sunday: 8am - 9pm

Semester break

Monday to Friday 8am - 5pm, closed on weekends

The library is closed on public holidays.

Address

Level 3, Beanland Building, RMIT Saigon South campus (702 Nguyen Van Linh boulevard, Tan Hung ward,

Ho Chi Minh city, Vietnam)

Contacts

Email:

library@rmit.edu.vn

Phone: (+84) 28 3776 1319

Wheelchair access

Wheelchair access is via lifts in Building 1, the Beanland Building.

Within the Library, Level 3 is accessible via wheelchair

Toilets are located near the entrance to The Beanland Library.
