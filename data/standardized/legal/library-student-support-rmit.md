The  library  offers  variety  of  online  and  offline  resources,  including  guides  and  workshops  to  assist  your

learning journey at RMIT Vietnam.

collections.  These  are  designed  to  help  you  make  the  most  of  the  library,  and  as  a  complement  to  other

student resources on offer at the university.
