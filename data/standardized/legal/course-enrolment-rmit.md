Discover how to enrol as a new or continuing student, as well as apply for credit or leave of absence.

Students are not required to be on campus to enrol; they just need to access the Enrolment tile in myRMIT to

do so.

Students can

log in to myRMIT

using either their Student ID (S-numbers) or RMIT email address (s-numbers@rmit.edu.vn).
