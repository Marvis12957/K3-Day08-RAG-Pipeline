Policy information for the Academic Achievement Scholarship, including value, eligibility and selection criteria.

The Academic Achievement Scholarship is awarded to scholarship applicants who have achieved exceptional academic results in high school. If chosen, you are offered the tuition fee reduction to the degree of your choice.
This annual scholarship is open to prospective students who meet all entry requirements for a Bachelor program at RMIT Vietnam.
Eligibility
To be eligible for this scholarship, you must:
Be a citizen of Vietnam
Have completed high school with a minimum grade 12 GPA of 8.5/10.0; or IB diploma with a minimum of 33 points in total; or GCE ‘A’ Levels (UK) of minimum 11 points for three Advanced subjects (A=5, B=4, C=3, D=2, E=1), or equivalent
Your high school graduation date must be within two years of the scholarship application deadline.
Have IELTS (Academic) 6.5+ (no band below 6.0) or equivalent. For other international English entry requirements, please refer to theEnglish equivalency requirements(links to Australian website)
Your IELTS test result must still be within its two-year validity period on the start date of your intended intake semester in 2026.
Have applied for admission to a Bachelor's degree program at RMIT Vietnam and meet the program entry requirements (conditional offer may be considered)
Commence study on your Bachelor's degree program in 2026. If successful, the scholarship will apply from semester 3 2026 (October 2026).
Selection criteria
Past academic performance is assessed by the selection committee and ranked according to the eligibility criteria. The top 11 applicants will receive a scholarship of 50% and the next 11 top-ranking applicants will receive a scholarship of 25%. Past academic performance is weighted as follows: 60% Year 12 GPA + 30% Year 11 GPA + 10% Year 10 GPA.
For Vietnamese high school transcripts that do not include a yearly cumulative GPA, the Scholarships Office will calculate the GPA for each year by averaging the GPA of the subjects assessed on the 10-point scale. Subjects assessed as pass/fail will not be included in the calculation.
